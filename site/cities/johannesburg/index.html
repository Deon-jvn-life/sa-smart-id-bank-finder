Johannesburg Smart ID branches

Checked against the DHA branch locator and the Digital Partnership page on 26 October 2026. Sources are linked next to each fact.

In short. Smart ID bank branches whose DHA city field is Johannesburg, as cards you can filter by bank. Soweto and Sandton are different city values.

24 Digital Partnership branches whose city field is Johannesburg in the DHA locator on 26 October 2026 [source]. Walk-in: DHA says Capitec and Standard Bank allow walk-ins without an appointment [source]. Booking: a Home Affairs office uses a different booking system.

This page keeps the city string the locator published. A branch filed under another city, including a suburb of the same metro, stays on the Gauteng page. Every locator row on 26 October 2026 was marked RE_ISSUE. No row listed a passport product code. The feed does not define what RE_ISSUE covers [source].

The cards on this page list every branch. This map is an extra view and only shows a pin where the official address could be placed. Map data is from OpenStreetMap. The page still scrolls as usual. Use the zoom buttons, or choose the map if you want the scroll wheel to zoom.

A map of the placed branches loads here if JavaScript is available. The cards are the full list.
