Nedbank: Smart ID and passport at branches

  • Not listed in the DHA Digital Partnership locator on 21 October 2026 [source].
  • Nedbank hosts DHA officials at participating branches (eHomeAffairs model): apply online, upload documents, pay online with Nedbank, book a date, then visit for biometrics [source].
  • Bring your booking confirmation and ID book or card [source]. DHA: no walk-ins at eHomeAffairs bank branches [source].
  • Collection: SMS when ready, collect at the branch you selected [source].
  • Children under 18: different requirements; Nedbank refers you to DHA [source].
  • Must you be a Nedbank client? Payment is described via Nedbank Money app or Online Banking; whether other payers are accepted is not stated by source.
  • Passports: Nedbank says DHA officials issue IDs and passports at these branches [source].
  • Extra Nedbank fee: not stated by source.
