Absa: Smart ID and passport at branches

  • Smart ID renewal at selected branches: book online, in the Absa Banking App (Menu, Government Services), or walk in [source].
  • Account: you must have an Absa Cheque or Savings account [source].
  • Payment: you pay at the branch, not online [source].
  • If booked, bring the SMS or email confirmation [source].
  • Extra Absa fee: not stated by source. DHA fee R140 [source].
  • Absa also runs a bank-based Home Affairs mobile unit [source].
  • Passports: not stated by source as currently available at Absa digital branches.
