FNB: Smart ID and passport at branches

  • Replace a Smart ID (lost, stolen, damaged) at selected branches, no appointment needed [source].
  • Apply or renew Smart ID or passport via eHomeAffairs: register and pay (FNB App or online banking, "Government Bills + Smart IDs"), book a slot, visit one of seven FNB branches with DHA representation [source].
  • FNB eHomeAffairs branches: Lynnwood (Grove Mall), Centurion Lifestyle, 4 Merchant Place, Green Point, Burgersfort, Cornubia, The Glen [source].
  • Eligibility for Smart ID replacement: FNB customers with an active, funded transactional account; South African citizen (birth or naturalisation) or permanent resident [source].
  • Payment: no cash; payments cannot be made in branch by cash, debit or credit card [source].
  • Documents: none needed for Smart ID replacement; bring original identity documents for eHomeAffairs appointments and collection [source].
  • Cost: Smart ID R140; FNB charges no additional fees [source].
  • Minors (eHomeAffairs route): passport, both parents; Smart ID, one parent [source].
