Standard Bank: Smart ID and passport at branches

In short. Standard Bank Smart ID and passport branch service: walk-in or booking, client requirement, fees and branch list. Sourced from the Department of Home Affairs.

  • In branch (Digital Partnership): green ID to Smart ID, Smart ID re-issue (lost, stolen, damaged), and Smart ID replacement due to marriage or divorce after the surname has been changed at a Home Affairs office. Walk-ins accepted [source].
  • Online (eHomeAffairs) route: first-time ID, Smart ID replacement and re-issue, first-time passport and passport renewal. Needs a Standard Bank Online Banking profile and country of birth South Africa; pay DHA via Online Banking, then book at a specialised branch [source].
  • Account: yes, you need a Standard Bank account [source].
  • Cost: Standard Bank will not charge customers during the launch phase [source]. DHA fee R140 [source].
  • Surname changes: cannot assist; visit Home Affairs [source]. The amendment routes, the fees, and the marriage or divorce replacement: Name or surname change.
  • Note: a 2025 gov.za statement said services would be open to non-clients [source]; Standard Bank's current page requires an account [source].
