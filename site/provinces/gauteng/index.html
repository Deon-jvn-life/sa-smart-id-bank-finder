Smart ID bank branches in Gauteng

In short. Bank branches in Gauteng for a Smart ID, from the Department of Home Affairs Digital Partnership locator and the older eHomeAffairs lists. Confirm the branch before you go.

187 Digital Partnership branches listed in the DHA locator on 26 October 2026 [source]. These handle Smart ID re-issues and green ID book conversions [source]. Check each bank's rules (most require you to be a client). Whether the green ID book is still valid, and what each bank says about that switch: Green ID book to Smart ID.
