Smart ID banks in Gauteng: Johannesburg and Pretoria

Checked against the DHA branch locator and the Digital Partnership page on 26 October 2026. Sources are linked next to each fact.

In short. Bank branches in Gauteng for a Smart ID, from the Department of Home Affairs Digital Partnership locator and the older eHomeAffairs lists. Confirm the branch before you go.

187 Digital Partnership branches in the DHA locator on 26 October 2026 [source]. Walk-in: DHA says Capitec and Standard Bank allow walk-ins without an appointment [source]. Booking: eHomeAffairs branches need a booking.

These locator rows are Smart ID re-issues and green ID book conversions [source]. Every row on 26 October 2026 was marked RE_ISSUE. No row listed a passport product code. The biggest city name people search for here is Johannesburg and Pretoria. City pages: JohannesburgPretoria. The green ID book deadline: Green ID book deadline.
