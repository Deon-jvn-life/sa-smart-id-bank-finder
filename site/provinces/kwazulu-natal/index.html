Smart ID bank branches in KwaZulu-Natal (Durban, SA)

Checked against the DHA branch locator and the Digital Partnership page on 26 October 2026. Sources are linked next to each fact.

In short. Bank branches in KwaZulu-Natal for a Smart ID, from the Department of Home Affairs Digital Partnership locator and the older eHomeAffairs lists. Confirm the branch before you go.

130 Digital Partnership branches in the DHA locator on 26 October 2026 [source]. Walk-in: DHA says Capitec and Standard Bank allow walk-ins without an appointment [source]. Booking: eHomeAffairs branches need a booking.

These locator rows are Smart ID re-issues and green ID book conversions [source]. Every row on 26 October 2026 was marked RE_ISSUE. No row listed a passport product code. The biggest city name people search for here is Durban. City pages: Durban. The green ID book deadline: Green ID book deadline.

The cards on this page list every branch. This map is an extra view and only shows a pin where the official address could be placed. Map data is from OpenStreetMap. The page still scrolls as usual. Use the zoom buttons, or choose the map if you want the scroll wheel to zoom.
